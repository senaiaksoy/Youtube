# Insuffisance ovarienne prématurée — source and recording brief

## Recommended title

`Insuffisance ovarienne prématurée : diagnostic, fertilité et traitement`

Alternative:

`IOP avant 40 ans : que signifie vraiment ce diagnostic ?`

Thumbnail:

`IOP AVANT 40 ANS`

## Target audience and promise

Francophone women younger than 40 with irregular or absent cycles, low AMH, suspected POI, or a new POI diagnosis. The video distinguishes low AMH from POI, explains diagnostic criteria and outlines fertility, hormone-replacement, long-term-health, and experimental-treatment discussions without promising an individual outcome.

| Diagnostic framework: disordered cycles for at least four months plus FSH >25 IU/L; repeat after 4-6 weeks if uncertainty | ESHRE/ASRM/IMS international POI guideline, 2024 | Pregnancy and effects of hormonal therapy must be considered. |
| AMH is not the primary diagnostic test for POI | ESHRE/ASRM/IMS international POI guideline, 2024 | Do not equate low AMH with POI or infertility. |
