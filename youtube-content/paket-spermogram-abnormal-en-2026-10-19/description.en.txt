THE NUMBERS MENTIONED IN THE VIDEO
WHO 2021 lower reference limits: the 5th centile of men whose partners got pregnant within a year. They are not a fertility cut-off, and some labs use other ranges, so always check the range printed on your own report.
• Semen volume: 1.4 mL
• Sperm concentration: 16 million/mL
• Total sperm number: 39 million per ejaculate
• Total motility: 42%
• Progressive motility: 30%
• Vitality: 54%
• Normal forms (morphology): 4%

The roadmap (UK NICE guideline, 2026)
• Two abnormal semen analyses: physical examination; hormone blood tests to be considered
• Concentration persistently below 5 million/mL: chromosome analysis (karyotype) to be considered
• No sperm at all, with no known cause: karyotype and Y-chromosome microdeletion test
• Below 1 million/mL, with no known cause: Y-chromosome microdeletion test
• Suspected blockage or absent vas deferens: CFTR (cystic fibrosis) gene test

THE THREE QUESTIONS TO ASK YOUR DOCTOR
1. Has the result been confirmed by a second test?
2. Do we need a physical exam and a hormone blood test?
3. Should we consult a urologist or an andrologist?

Sudden, severe pain in a testicle is an emergency: go to the emergency department or call 911 (US), 999 (UK), 112 (EU) or your local emergency number. A lump in a testicle: see a doctor promptly.
