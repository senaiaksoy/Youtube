# Third-party sources

Worlds and the scene kit were written for this library. No code was copied from the projects below; they informed the look and the motion ideas. `templates.json` names the item behind each world.

| Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| [heygen-com/hyperframes](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) registry | Apache-2.0 | Ideas for gazete, isvicre, afis, kolaj, lab-hud, cam, sinema, pastel. `catalog.json` is a metadata snapshot of the registry. Items installed with `hyperframes add` keep their own licence notice. |
| [nateherkai/herk-edit-kit](https://github.com/nateherkai/herk-edit-kit) | MIT (brand assets of AI Automation Society excluded) | Idea for milimetrik-gece. No brand asset was used. |
| [reactvideoeditor/hyperframes-templates](https://github.com/reactvideoeditor/hyperframes-templates) | MIT | Reviewed as a source of chart blocks. Nothing taken yet. |
| [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org) 5.3.0 | SIL OFL 1.1 | Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono, Playfair Display, Inter Tight, Anton, Fraunces, Archivo, Manrope, DM Serif Display, Nunito. Licences in `fonts/`. |

Reviewed and not used:

- hyperframer.com/bookmark/templates: Framer website templates, no video templates, no stated licence.
- ali-abassi/remotion-templates, reactvideoeditor/remotion-templates, huntingrin/make-90s-tutorial-video: no licence file.
- `hyperframes templates`: HyperFrames Pro, paid.
