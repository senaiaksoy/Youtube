CoQ10 before IVF: promising, but not proven to increase live births. Evidence Score: 3/4.

In a randomized trial of women under 35 with low ovarian reserve, 60 days of CoQ10 before IVF led to more eggs and more good-quality embryos. Meta-analyses report higher clinical pregnancy rates, but live-birth rates were not significantly different, and the trials come from a small number of centers.
