TITLE (44 chars): Clomid: How Many Cycles Is Too Many? #Shorts

DESCRIPTION:
How long should you keep trying Clomid (clomiphene)? The French prescribing information sets a maximum of six treatment cycles, and if three courses at the recommended dose do not produce ovulation, the trial is considered over. If ovulation happens but there is still no pregnancy after several tries, the workup and the strategy are reviewed.

TITLE (55 chars): Clomid Safety: Vision Changes and Warning Signs #Shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Clomid (clomiphene) warning signs: blurred vision, flashes of light or spots in front of your eyes mean stopping the treatment and contacting your doctor promptly. Severe abdominal pain, rapid swelling of the belly or shortness of breath is a medical emergency; in an emergency call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 999 in the UK). Before every new treatment cycle, a pregnancy must be ruled out.
