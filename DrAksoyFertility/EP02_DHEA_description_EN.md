TITLE:
DHEA for IVF: Does It Really Increase Live Births? What 28 Trials Show

DESCRIPTION:
Does DHEA improve your chances of having a baby with IVF? Dr Senai Aksoy, Associate Professor of Reproductive Medicine, looks at what the studies actually measured: who was studied (poor responders, low ovarian reserve), and which outcome was reported, from more eggs and embryos to clinical pregnancy and live birth.

In short: current evidence does not show that DHEA increases live births in IVF, and the ESHRE ovarian stimulation guideline does not recommend it before or during stimulation.
