DESCRIPTION:
Taking Clomid (clomiphene) but still not pregnant? Dr Senai Aksoy, Associate Professor of Reproductive Medicine, starts with the question that decides what comes next: did the treatment actually trigger ovulation? Not ovulating and ovulating without getting pregnant are two different situations, and each needs a different next step.

In short: the French prescribing information sets a maximum of six treatment cycles, and three courses at the recommended dose without ovulation means the trial is over. Clomiphene has not been shown to improve egg quality, and used alone in women who already ovulate with unexplained infertility it has not shown benefit. For ovulation problems caused by PCOS, with no other fertility factor, the 2023 international guideline places letrozole first line. Warning signs matter too: vision changes mean stopping the treatment and contacting your doctor.

If you have blurred vision, flashes of light, spots in front of your eyes, severe abdominal pain, rapid swelling of the belly or shortness of breath, contact your doctor straight away.
