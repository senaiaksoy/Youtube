## Açıklama

When can you see the heartbeat at the first ultrasound after IVF, and what if there's no cardiac activity yet? Dr Senai Aksoy, Associate Professor of Reproductive Medicine, explains what the doctor looks for, week by week.

In short: cardiac activity is usually visible at around 6 weeks of pregnancy. Too early is not necessarily a bad sign: a follow-up scan at least a week later is often planned before drawing any conclusion.

Inside: how weeks of pregnancy are counted after an embryo transfer, what a transvaginal scan shows and when, the criteria for a follow-up scan, what the studies say about pregnancies that continue, ectopic pregnancy, and the warning signs that should never wait for your next appointment.
