**Title:** Periods Every Three Months: No Risk of Pregnancy? #Shorts

Irregular periods while on a weight-loss injection: "I'm not going to get pregnant"? Your body ovulates first, and the period comes later, so a pregnancy can happen when you least expect it. There are not enough human data to say these medications are safe during pregnancy; effective contraception is essential during treatment and during each drug's washout period.
