## TITLE
Weight-Loss Injections and Pregnancy: Do They Really Help You Get Pregnant?

## DESCRIPTION
"One shot, zero appetite…" then "surprise, I got pregnant": on social media, the link between weight-loss injections and pregnancy looks obvious. Dr Senai Aksoy, Associate Professor of Reproductive Medicine, looks at what the studies actually show about GLP-1 medications (semaglutide, liraglutide, tirzepatide) and fertility.

In short: these medications may contribute indirectly to pregnancy chances in some women, particularly with PCOS. They are not direct infertility treatments, and they are not used during pregnancy. We also cover how long to stop them before a planned pregnancy, contraception, egg retrieval and anesthesia, and what is known in men.
